=== NexaDesk v1.0 Pricing Tiers ===

[Monthly Subscription Tiers]
Free Community: Free for up to 2 agents, 100 tickets per month.
Starter Tier: $19 per agent per month, unlimited tickets, standard email support.
Pro Tier: $49 per agent per month, custom email domain, priority queue.

Billing Policy: All billing is month-to-month. Annual pre-payment discounts are not offered in v1.0.
