# NexaDesk v1.0 Reporting and Analytics

## Manual Report Exports in v1.0

NexaDesk v1.0 supports manual report exports only.

Administrators must manually click the 'Export CSV' button on the ticket ledger page to download ticket history.
Supported format: Raw CSV format only.
Scheduled report delivery, recurring email reports, and automated background exports are NOT supported in v1.0.
