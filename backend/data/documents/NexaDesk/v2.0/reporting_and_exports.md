# NexaDesk v2.0 Reporting and Analytics

## Scheduled Report Exports in v2.0

NexaDesk v2.0 introduces scheduled report exports.

Team managers can set up automated recurring schedules: Daily, Weekly on Mondays, or Monthly on the 1st.
Reports are automatically compiled in the background and delivered via email to designated management distribution lists.
Supported formats in v2.0: CSV and PDF summary report.
Real-time AI automated anomaly prediction is not supported in v2.0.
